# Demo frame provenance

`frame-wxy2048.jpg`, `frame-vbn7712.jpg`, `frame-jsa401.jpg` and `frame-bqq993.jpg` are derived from `assets/images/dashcam-evidence-demo.jpg`, the project's AI-generated synthetic demo frame. That frame shows a blank plate.

Each file composites a synthetic, legible Malaysian-style plate (white characters on black, Big Shoulders Display 800, 0.6 px blur and a light gradient to match the lighting) at 964,614 (152×40 px). The frames were rendered with headless Chrome at 1672×941 and saved as JPEG quality 70.

The plates are fictional demo fixtures (`WXY 2048`, `VBN 7712`, `JSA 401`, `BQQ 993`) that match `variants/shared/core2.js`. They show no real vehicle. Replace them with stills from the actual stage clip and backend track data.
